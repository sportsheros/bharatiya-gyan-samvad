# 1. PRODUCT VISION — WHAT ARE WE BUILDING?

We are building **Bharatiya Gyan Samvad (भारतीय ज्ञान संवाद)**, an Indian Knowledge and Spirituality Media Brand.

This is not just a religious website, a personal blog, or a single-person podcast website.

Our long-term vision is to build a trusted digital media platform that explores India's ancient knowledge, philosophy, scriptures, traditions, and spiritual wisdom through meaningful conversations.

Our central idea is:

**"जहाँ शास्त्र मिलते हैं जीवन से।"**

We want to understand traditional Indian knowledge and connect it with the questions, challenges, decisions, and experiences of modern human life.

Examples:

- What can the Bhagavad Gita teach us about stress and decision-making?
- What do the Upanishads say about understanding ourselves?
- What can the Ramayana teach us about relationships, leadership, and responsibilities?
- How can Indian philosophy help us understand the purpose of life?
- What is the difference between traditional beliefs, philosophical interpretations, and scientifically established facts?

Our content should be accessible to ordinary people, not only scholars or religious experts.

# 2. IMPORTANT: OUR FOUNDER'S ROLE AND APPROACH

This is one of the most important aspects of the brand.

The founder is NOT presenting himself as a Sanskrit scholar, religious authority, philosopher, or subject-matter expert.

The founder is beginning this initiative as a:

- Learner
- Researcher
- Curious student of Indian knowledge
- Discussion host
- Knowledge communicator

The founder's intention is to study, understand, ask questions, research authentic sources, and share meaningful learning with the audience.

**We believe that learning should come before teaching and understanding should come before explaining.**

We do not want to pretend to know everything.

We want to create a platform where knowledge is explored through curiosity, study, research, and respectful dialogue.

Our approach:

1. First, study a topic.
2. Read relevant original texts and reliable translations.
3. Understand the context and different interpretations.
4. Identify questions and doubts.
5. Research the topic further.
6. Prepare a structured discussion.
7. Create a podcast or video.
8. Share the learning with the audience.

When a topic requires deeper subject expertise, we will consult or invite knowledgeable people and experts.

We will clearly distinguish between:
- What the original text says.
- What a traditional interpretation says.
- What a scholar or expert explains.
- What our own understanding or interpretation is.
- What is scientifically established and what is not.

We will never claim expertise that we do not possess.

# 3. OUR CONTENT CREATION MODEL

Our content creation process will follow this cycle:

**Study → Research → Understand → Prepare → Discuss → Publish**

## Step 1: Daily Study

The founder will dedicate time regularly to studying Indian knowledge and philosophy.

Possible study subjects:

- Bhagavad Gita
- Vedas
- Upanishads
- Ramayana
- Mahabharata
- Indian philosophy
- Ayurveda and traditional knowledge
- Bhakti and spirituality
- Indian history and cultural traditions

## Step 2: Research and Verification

After studying a topic, we will research it further.

We will:
- Refer to original texts wherever possible.
- Use reliable translations and commentaries.
- Compare interpretations when necessary.
- Record important references, chapters, and verses.
- Avoid presenting unsupported claims as facts.

## Step 3: Understanding and Notes

Before creating content, prepare notes covering:

- What is the topic?
- What does the source actually say?
- What is its historical or philosophical context?
- What are the important questions?
- How does it relate to modern life?
- What is still unclear and needs further research?

## Step 4: Podcast Preparation

Once the topic is sufficiently understood, prepare a discussion outline.

The outline may include:
- Introduction to the topic.
- Relevant source references.
- Important questions.
- Practical examples.
- Different interpretations.
- Modern-day relevance.
- Summary and key learning.

## Step 5: Podcast / Samvad

The initial format may involve:

- A host (founder).
- A co-host who asks practical questions.
- A knowledgeable guest or subject expert when required.

The conversation should feel natural, thoughtful, and educational.

The host does not need to pretend to be an expert. Asking genuine questions is an important part of the format.

## Step 6: Publishing

Initial content distribution:

- YouTube: Full podcast and educational videos.
- Instagram: Short clips, important insights, and selected learning highlights.
- Facebook: Videos, short clips, and educational posts.

### Important publishing principle

Daily study is our internal preparation and learning process.

We do not have to publish every day's study.

Only researched, understood, and properly prepared content should become an official podcast or educational publication.

We may introduce a public learning journey in the future, but it is not a requirement for the initial launch.
